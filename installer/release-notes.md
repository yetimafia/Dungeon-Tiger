**Dungeon Tiger 1.3.1: prettier sprites**
- 🎨 Every monster and hero has been repainted to look nicer:
  - **Shading:** shadows lean cool and highlights lean warm, the way pixel artists colour things. Clean bands of shading, with dithering only where two bands meet. Smooth shading without stripes.
  - **Outlines:** in a dark version of each colour instead of plain black, with a soft shadow on the ground.
  - **Light:** a cool rim light on the shadow side, contact shadows where parts overlap, and a shine on metal.
  - **People:** rounder bodies, with shoulders wider than the waist, tapered arms and legs, boots, and slightly bigger heads.
  - **Beasts:** tapered legs with paws and claws.
- The battle map and the players' website use the new sprites.
